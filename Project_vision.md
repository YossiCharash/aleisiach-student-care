### What we're building
A cloud system to manage and track students in a special-needs educational/therapeutic
setting ("Aleisiach"). For each student the system centralizes: a personal program
(strength areas and areas to strengthen), documentation of monthly team meetings, social
worker notes, and full student details (identity, guardianship, diagnoses, and more).

### For whom (roles)
- **Instructor (מדריך)** — an educational worker who sees only the students in their own class.
- **Professional teacher (מורה מקצועי/ת)** — sees all students, but only part of the tabs/data.
- **Manager (מנהל)** — full access to all students + a Settings page (taxonomy & password management).
- **Social worker (עו"ס)** — write access to the social worker note (Tab 3). *(To decide: is
  "social worker" a separate role or a sub-permission of manager.)*

### Product principles
1. **Clear Hebrew, full RTL.** All copy, direction, and layout in Hebrew.
2. **Desktop only** — no mobile design needed at this stage.
3. **Professional and modern look, not "generic AI".** Match colors and typography to
   https://aleisiach.org/ (Aleisiach branding).
4. **Privacy & sensitive data.** This concerns minors with special needs, medical diagnoses
   and guardianship details — highly sensitive data. Security and permissions are a core
   requirement, not an add-on.
5. **Configurable taxonomy.** The team-meeting form is built dynamically from labels →
   sub-labels → skills → solutions, all defined on the Settings page and never hard-coded.
